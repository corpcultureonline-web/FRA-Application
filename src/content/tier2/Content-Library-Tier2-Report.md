## 1. What this screen is, and what it is not

**It is the product.** Everything before it is a transaction; this is the thing he paid ₹2,359 for. He will read it more than once, show it to a partner or an accountant, and decide from it whether to spend ₹7,079 on the Roadmap.

**It is not a new document.** The Report already exists — thirteen blocks, generated by the engine, approved by Ronak, and rendered as the PDF that has been on the landing page for a week. **This screen is that document on the web**, plus four things a PDF cannot do.

### The four differences from the PDF

| | |
|---|---|
| **Section 02 can be absent** | A brand with no contradictions has no Section 02. **Section 05 is always present** *(T98)* — a brand with clean legals gets five gates marked Clear, not a missing section. **The layout must read as finished at either length** |
| **The Roadmap is a button**, not a printed URL | Section 10 is the Tier 3 sale. On paper it is a web address nobody types |
| **The contents list is navigation** | Nine PDF pages is a long web page. "What is inside" stops being a list and becomes how he moves |
| **It is readable on a phone** | Which a nine-page A4 PDF is not. This is the single biggest reason the web version exists |

**Everything else is the same document.** Same blocks, same order, same words.

## 2. Where the words come from — and the one rule that matters

**Almost nothing on this screen is written here.**

| Part | Source |
|---|---|
| Every block of the Report itself | The engine, assembling `Content-Library-Group-A` … `-H` from the scores |
| The score, band, pillar figures, gap ranking | The engine |
| Section 10's Roadmap offer | Group H and `CANONICAL-VALUES.md` §1 |
| **The page's own chrome** — download, navigation, states | **This file. `T2R-` keys** |

### The rule: render what Ronak approved, not what the library says

**If the reviewer edited a block, this page shows the edited text** *(T70)*. The PDF does too. A page that renders the library version while the PDF carries the edit would hand a founder two different Reports from one link.

**The engine's output is frozen at approval.** Re-running the assembly when the page loads would mean a Report that changes between readings, which contradicts the "final, does not change" promise printed on it.

## 4. Absence — the real design problem

**Sections disappear. Nothing announces that they have.**

| Section | Absent when |
|---|---|
| **02 · Where Else Your Answers Disagree** | No contradictions fired. **Common — most brands have one or two, some have none** |
| ~~**05 · Non-Negotiables**~~ | **No longer vanishes** *(T98, 20 October)*. Section 05 lists every gate, clean ones included, in one of three states *(T103)*. Only Section 02 can be absent |
| **The band-margin note in 03** | The score is not within a point of a boundary *(D24)*. **Usually absent** |
| **The "Against your free result" panel in 03** | Never absent, but its content is generated per brand |

**Section 05 carries five rows, always — one per gate**, and each row is `T2R-CLEAR`, `T2R-INPROGRESS` or `T2R-MUSTRESOLVE` *(T103)*. **None is ever omitted.** Row titles are `Content-Library-Group-I.md` §6.5; block text is its §4. **The strip's fourth tile counts the Must resolve rows only** — an In progress row is not a blocker and must not inflate the count.


**No placeholder, ever.** No "this section does not apply to you", no greyed heading, no empty card. A missing section is missing, and the contents list simply does not list it.

**Every cross-reference to Section 02 must be conditional.** A note reading "see Section 02" points at nothing for a brand with no contradictions. Either the note drops with the section, or it is reworded to name the thing rather than the number. **This is a sweep, not a single fix.** *(Design, v37.)* **Section 05 references need no such guard since T98** — the section is always there.

### Absence is not the same as emptiness — where the good news goes

**A brand that loses a section has earned something, and the page has to say so.** Layout alone cannot: a page that runs 04 → 06 in silence reads as a page that failed to load, however well it is set.

But the sentence may not announce the absence, because that reintroduces the placeholder by the back door. **It states a result, in a place that is always present.**

| Key | Copy | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `T2R-NOBLOCKERS` | Nothing | The glance strip's fourth tile, in place of a count |
| `T2R-NOBLOCKERS-NOTE` | No legal or structural blockers. | Beneath it |
| `T2R-NOCONTRA` | Nothing you told us contradicts anything else you told us. | The end of Section 03 |

**`T2R-NOCONTRA` named the wrong finding in v1.1 and is corrected here.** The old wording said nothing contradicted the *free Audit*. Section 02 is not about the Audit — it is about the founder's answers disagreeing **with each other**, and a brand can have no Section 02 while having moved a long way from its Audit result. The two are independent, and the line now says only what the absence of Section 02 actually proves. *(Design, v39.)*

**It also moves out of the comparison panel**, which is about the Audit, to the end of Section 03 — always present, and topically right, because Section 03 is where the quality of the inputs is discussed.

**This overrides v1.0: the strip does not drop to three tiles when nothing is outstanding.** The fourth tile stays and reads **Must resolve — Nothing**. It reports a finding instead of leaving a hole, it holds the layout, and for that brand it is the strongest sentence on the page.

**The three-tile strip is retired entirely** *(T102)*. v1.0 kept it as a fallback for a tile with no value to show; **since T98 the tile always has a value, because nought is a value.** There is no state left that produces three tiles, and a board drawn for one is a board for an unreachable case. **The strip is four tiles on every board, at every width.**

**The numbering does not renumber.** If Section 02 is absent the page runs 01, 03, 04 — because the founder may compare his Report with another brand's, and because the PDF, the review panel and the content library all use these numbers as names. **A renumbered Report is a different document from the one Ronak approved.**

### The case that will look worst

**A brand with no contradictions loses one section.** The page runs 01 → 03 → 04 → 05 → 06 → 07 → 08 → 09 → 10, and the join between 01 and 03 is the one place it can read as though something failed to load.

**Draw that case.** For the strongest possible brand it pairs with a Section 05 of five Clear rows and a fourth tile reading Nothing — **and it must feel like good news rather than a short Report.** *(Corrected from a two-section version that T98 had already made impossible.)*

### Not assessed — the state nobody has drawn

**A pillar with fewer than three answers prints no score** *(T46)*. It is named "not assessed", excluded from the gap ranking, and it appears that way in **two** places: the bar chart in Section 03 and its own block in Section 06.

**It needs a visual that is neither a zero nor a blank.** A bar with no fill reads as a score of nought, which is the opposite of what happened.

| Key | Copy |
|---|---|
| `T2R-NOTASSESSED` | Not assessed |
| `T2R-NOTASSESSED-WHY` | Too few answers to score this area. Your Report says what that means. |

**The Section 06 block for a pillar that was not assessed** — `T2R-NOTASSESSED-BLOCK`, three paragraphs in place of the usual three:

> **{Pillar} — not assessed**
>
> You marked most of the questions in this area as something you don't track, or that doesn't apply to you. That is a legitimate answer, and we would rather leave this blank than print a score built on two replies.
>
> It is excluded from your overall score and from the ranking in Section 04 — your score is the average of what you did tell us, not an average with a hole in it.
>
> If this area matters to your franchise plan, it is the first thing to come back to, because as things stand neither of us knows where it sits.

**The "what you told us" list stays under it**, showing the declines. It is the useful part: he sees exactly which questions he passed on, which is the only way the last paragraph turns into an action.

**The tone is neutral, not corrective.** Validation is mandatory and nothing is skippable, so a pillar only reaches this state because the founder explicitly declined three or more of its questions. He chose this knowingly and the copy should not read as though he failed to do something.

**A declined answer appears in the list as the question label and the answer exactly as the founder saw it** — *"Average Google rating — We don't have a Google listing"*. Never paraphrased, never "not answered". The list exists so he can verify the Report is about him, and a paraphrase of his own decline is the one thing he cannot verify. *(Design, v39.)*

### T46 cannot currently fire, and stays anyway *(T81)*

**Design checked the question set and found the state unreachable. That is correct.** Only seven questions carry an escape answer — `UE7`, `UE9`, `BP3`, `BP4`, `G2`, `G4`, `G5` — and the three `G` escapes are flag-only on the legal questions, so they never reduce a count. **Two NULL-producing escapes sit in Profit & Payback, two in Brand Pull, and none in the other five pillars.** Profit & Payback bottoms out at four answers of six, Brand Pull at three of five, and the threshold is *fewer than three*. Audit answers add to the count, so they push it further out of reach.

**The rule stays, and the board stays with it.** T46 is a guard, not a feature: it exists so that a change to the question set cannot quietly produce a pillar score built on one answer in front of a paying customer. Guards that never fire are working.

**It gets a test rather than a comment.** Added to the band-edge test cases already outstanding: *assert that the minimum achievable answer count for every pillar is at least three.* The day someone adds an eighth escape, that test fails and tells them T46 just went live — which is the only reliable way this gets noticed a year from now.

**Design's board is therefore a layout case, correctly labelled.** It costs one board and it is the cheapest insurance on the page.

## 5. The blocks, and what each needs on the web

### Cover and score

The brand name, the meta line, the big number, the band badge, the five-segment scale and `T2R-FINAL`.

**On a phone the scale is the hard part.** Five bands across 390px with the founder's position marked. It carries the single most important piece of information on the page and it cannot become a legend.

**No range. Anywhere.** R12: the Report is one precise number. The Audit's range appears only inside Section 03's comparison panel, as history.

**The cover paragraph in the generated text currently breaks this** — it reads *"Your free result put you between 57 and 74"*. **That sentence is deleted from the engine text, not kept because the PDF has it** *(Design, v37)*. The cover is where a range costs most: it sits inches from `T2R-FINAL`, and the first screen either establishes the Report's authority or undercuts it. Section 03 keeps the comparison, because that section is explicitly about the free result. **The sample PDF regenerates with this change.**

### The glance strip — four tiles

Your score · Costliest gap · Strongest pillar · Must resolve.

**Stacks on a phone.** The fourth tile stays at four even with no non-negotiables — see `T2R-NOBLOCKERS` in §4. **It keys off a count of nought, never off a missing Section 05**, which since T98 cannot happen.

### Every figure in the strip is derived, never written

**Strongest pillar is the highest `pillarScore`. Costliest gap is the top of the Section 04 ranking. Must resolve counts Section 05's outstanding rows** — the ones marked `T2R-MUSTRESOLVE`, never all five.** If the strip can disagree with the section beneath it, the strip is a bug.

### The captions are templates, not sentences

**Each tile carries a label, a value and a caption, and the caption is a template with derived slots** — the engine fills it, it never composes it. *(Design, v39: two were left as placeholders because the derivation rule covered figures but not the line beneath them.)*

| Tile | Value | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| Your score | `{overall} / 100` | `{band}` |
| Costliest gap | `{pillar}` | `Scored {score}, at {weight}% of your result.` |
| Strongest pillar | `{pillar}` | `Scored {score}, at {weight}% of your result.` |
| Must resolve | `{n} items` — **`1 item` at one** *(T124)* | `{Section 05 row titles, joined}.` |

Chai Junction, in full: **68.8 / Almost Ready · Profit & Payback / "Scored 60.0, at 20% of your result." · Systems / "Scored 90.0, at 20% of your result." · 2 items / "Your business entity and your licences."**

**Showing the weight is the point of the caption.** Costliest gap and strongest pillar both carry 20% here and sit thirty points apart, which says more in two short lines than a paragraph would. The weights are already printed in Section 03's table, so nothing new is disclosed.

**Joining the must-resolve titles:** up to three are listed in full; beyond three, two are listed and the rest become `and {n} more`. **When no gate is outstanding — Section 05 present, every row Clear — the tile takes `T2R-NOBLOCKERS` instead.** The trigger is a must-resolve count of nought, not an absent section *(T101)*.

**This rule exists because the sample broke it twice at once.** Design found the strip naming *Your Role (88.0)* as strongest when *Systems* scored **90.0**, and naming *"no partner profile, no selection process"* as must-resolve when Section 05 holds the business entity and the licences. The second is the more instructive error: Right Partner scoring 20.0 is the worst **score** on the Report, and a worst score is not a non-negotiable. The two were authored separately from the sections they summarise, which is the only way they could ever have diverged.

### What is inside — the contents list

**On the web this is navigation.** Each row jumps to its section. It lists only the sections that exist.

**It should be reachable again from anywhere** — `T2R-TOP`, or a persistent way back. Nine pages of scrolling with no way home is the commonest failure of a long web document.

### 03 · How Your Score Is Built

Seven rows: pillar, bar, score, weight, status. **This is a table, and tables are where mobile layouts fail.** It must stay scannable at 320px without becoming a horizontal scroll.

Then the "Against your free result" panel — generated per brand, always present.

### 06 · The Seven Pillars, in Writing

**The longest section by far** — seven blocks, each with a score, three paragraphs and a list of the answers behind it. On a phone this is most of the reading.

**The "what you told us" list under each pillar is the thing founders check.** It is where they verify the Report is about them. It must not be collapsed by default.

### 10 · What Comes Next — the only place this page sells

The Roadmap, at **₹5,999 + 18% GST, ₹7,079 payable**, from config and never as a literal.

**One button.** `T2R-CTA` is the only call to action on the page. P4: the Roadmap is offered after the Report, never before — this is the after.

**It must not feel like an advertisement bolted to a diagnosis.** Section 09 has just said plainly what this Report does *not* include; Section 10 is the answer to that sentence, and it reads best as a continuation rather than a pitch.

## 6. What must not appear

- **No share buttons.** The link is private *(T21)*
- **No range, no "approximately", nothing suggesting the score could move.** It says final because it is *(R12)*
- **No print stylesheet fighting the PDF.** The PDF is the artefact for printing; the page is for reading
- **No "last updated" or version number.** A Report is dated once, when it is sent
- **No comparison to other brands** beyond what Section 08 already says *(B4)*
- **No re-sell of the Report itself.** He has it
- **Nothing that depends on the founder being logged in.** The link is the credential

## 9. The link does not expire while we hold the Report *(T80, qualified)*

**Screen 7's expiry pattern does not carry across, and Design was right to question it.** Nothing here is at risk of being lost: his Report exists, it is final, and "Your answers are safe" answers a question he is not asking.

**Expiry would also cost us the behaviour we most want.** A founder re-reading his Report three times over a fortnight before deciding on the Roadmap is precisely what `visit_n` exists to measure, and it is the strongest buying signal in the funnel. A link that dies at thirty days turns that founder into a support request. The page is private because the link is private *(T21)*; the PDF is the artefact built to be forwarded.

**So there is no expired state.** What remains is a link that resolves to nothing — mistyped, truncated by a mail client, invented, or belonging to a Report we no longer hold.

### The qualification, which v1.3 was missing

**"Never expires" meant no artificial expiry window — not that the Report is kept forever**, and as written it contradicted the Privacy Policy, which retains Reports for three years from last activity *(Design, v44)*.

**Both are right and neither moves.** Data minimisation under the DPDP Act means we cannot keep a founder's answers indefinitely, and a thirty-day link is still the wrong answer for a document people re-read before buying the Roadmap. **Three years is long enough that no real founder meets a dead link, and finite enough to be lawful.**

**The erasure case is the one that reaches this page**, and the Privacy Policy already warns about it: ask us to erase your data and the link stops working, so download the PDF first.

| Key | Copy |
|---|---|
| `T2R-UNKNOWN-HEAD` | We can't find that Report. |
| `T2R-UNKNOWN-SUB` | The link may be incomplete. Try opening it again from your email or WhatsApp. |
| `T2R-UNKNOWN-HELP` | Still stuck? Write to bd@corpculture.co and we'll look into it. |

**Both sentences used to promise more than we can keep** *(Design, v44)*. The old `-SUB` said *"your Report is still there"* and the old `-HELP` said *"we'll send it again"* — **neither is true for a Report that has passed its retention period and been erased**, which is precisely one of the cases that lands on this page. The page now says what it knows: the link didn't resolve, and here is who to ask.

**It offers no "send me a new link" button**, which is Design's second point and the sharper one: an unrecognised token has no order behind it, so there is no address to send anything to. A button that cannot work is worse than no button.

**The page deliberately reassures less than it used to.** An earlier draft told the founder his Report was still there and that we would send it again, which reads better and is sometimes false — the erased-Report case lands here too. **"Try opening it again from your email or WhatsApp" is as far as the page can honestly go**, and the help line carries the rest.

## 10. Rules

**Customer-facing names are the Audit, the Report, the Roadmap.** Never "Tier 1/2/3" *(P17)*.

**Prices render from config** — `CANONICAL-VALUES.md` §1 — never as literals.

**The page shows the approved version, including any reviewer edits** *(T70)*. It is never re-assembled at load.

**The PDF and this page are the same Report.** If they can ever differ, the bug is serious: a founder forwards the PDF to his accountant and discusses the page with us.
