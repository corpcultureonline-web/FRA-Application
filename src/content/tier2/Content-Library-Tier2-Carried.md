**Version 1.2 · 13 October 2026 · Screen 3 complete and approved**

---

## 1. What this screen is for

**It is a reassurance screen, not a content screen.** The founder has just paid. The next thing they see should confirm that the money bought something and that their earlier effort was not wasted.

**One screen. No scrolling on a phone, if it can be helped.** Everything here is a reminder; nothing needs reading twice.

---

## 2. The copy

| Key | Copy |
|---|---|
| `T2W-HEAD` | What you already told us |
| `T2W-SUB` | You answered eleven questions in the Audit. All of it carries into your Report — we will not ask again. |
| `T2W-RANGE-LABEL` | Your Audit range |
| `T2W-RANGE-NOTE` | The Report replaces this range with one number. |
| `T2W-START` | **Start the questions** |
| `T2W-TIME` | About thirty more questions, roughly 15 minutes. Your answers save as you go. |

### The eleven answers themselves

**Design must not write these, and neither may we.** Each is a restatement generated from the founder's own Audit answer, held as `restatement` keys in the scoring config — **and the same strings are live on the Audit result page.** Rewriting one to fit this screen changes a published page.

### There are seven sentences, not eleven — and that is correct

Eleven questions are answered; several combine into one sentence because they read better that way. *"Your outlets earn back their cost in 18 to 24 months, at a margin of 10 to 15%"* is two answers in one line, and splitting it into two bullets would be worse reading for no gain.

**So `T2W-SUB` counts the questions, not the sentences.** "You answered eleven questions" is true, and nothing on screen contradicts it because there is nothing to count. The earlier wording — *"These eleven answers…"* — invited a count that would have come up short. Corrected.

Up to **two further lines** appear when the legal gates apply, so the list runs **7 to 9 items**.

### Length — no cap

**The longest live restatement runs about 125 characters**, which is **three lines at 390px** — measured on the built board, 13 October, correcting the two lines stated here before. At 320px it is more; Design states the measured figure rather than leaving an inference in the file.

**Design accommodates that; we do not shorten the copy to fit.** A 55-character cap would make every answer one line on a phone and would also reduce these sentences to telegraphic fragments that lose the qualifier doing the work — *"most core workflows have an SOP a new manager could follow unassisted"* is not the same claim as *"most workflows documented"*.

**The no-scrolling goal was an aspiration, not a requirement.** If it costs copy quality it is dropped. A short scroll on a three-second reassurance screen costs nothing; a mangled restatement of the founder's own business costs trust at the moment they have just paid.

## 3. Correcting an answer — settled: option B

**The founder will want to.** Seeing eleven statements about their own business is exactly the moment someone notices one is wrong, or that they misread a question four minutes in.

**Three options:**

| | What happens | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Read-only, no route** | Nothing. They proceed with an answer they know is wrong | Cheapest to build; worst answer. The Report is then built on a figure they already distrust, and they will say so when it arrives |
| **B. Read-only, with a way to flag it** | A quiet line offering to correct it. It reaches the team, who fix it before Ronak reviews the Report | Small build, honest. **Chosen** |
| **C. Editable in place** | They change the answer and the carried scores recompute | Most work, and it lets a founder tune their own inputs after seeing which way the score moves |

**Settled: B.** The Audit answers feed the Report's area scores, so an edit made after payment changes the result mid-flight. Option C also quietly invites gaming — a founder who has seen their range and now sees the inputs can work out which answer to change.

**Copy for B — settled 12 October**, by acceptance of the built screen rather than by separate decision:

| Key | Copy | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `T2W-FIX` | Something not right? Tell us and we will correct it before Ronak reviews your Report. | **Approved 13 October.** |

It links to the same WhatsApp number and email as everywhere else. **"Before Ronak reviews your Report" is deliberate** — it tells the founder there is a window rather than a permanent record. *(It is no longer the refund boundary — T72 moved that to submission — so the two sentences must not be reworded into each other.)*

## 4. Rules

**No price on this screen.** They have paid. Showing the amount again invites a second thought about it.

**No upsell, no Roadmap mention, no feature list.** This screen looks backwards for three seconds and then forwards.

**Customer-facing names are the Audit, the Report, the Roadmap.** Never "Tier 1/2/3".
