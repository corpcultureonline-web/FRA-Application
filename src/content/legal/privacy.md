## 2. The short version

**We collect what you type into the Audit and the Report questionnaire, your email address and mobile number, and a record of how you use what we send you.** We use it to produce your Report, deliver it, and talk to you about it. We don't sell it, we don't publish it, and we don't show it to other brands.

## 3. What we collect

### What you tell us

- **Your answers.** Eleven questions in the free Audit, about thirty more in the Report questionnaire. These describe your business — revenue, margins, outlets, staff, systems, legal position
- **Your brand name** and the category you operate in
- **Your email address**, which you give at the end of the free Audit
- **Your mobile number**, which you give at checkout, used to send your Report on WhatsApp
- **Anything you write to us** by email or WhatsApp

### What we record automatically

We keep an event log of what happens in the product: when an audit is started and finished, when a payment succeeds or fails, when a questionnaire is submitted, when a Report is generated, reviewed, approved and sent, and what happens to it afterwards. §5 covers that last part.

We also record the usual technical information a website receives — your browser type, your approximate location from your IP address, and the pages you open.

### What we get from the payment

**We never see your card number, UPI PIN, or bank credentials.** Payments go through Razorpay, who handle them on their own systems. We receive the amount, the date, whether it succeeded, a payment reference, and the name and contact details you gave Razorpay.

## 5. Your Report link, and what we can see

**This section exists because we would rather tell you than have you discover it.**

When your Report is ready we send you a private link, by email and on WhatsApp. That link is yours alone. **We record how it is used**, specifically:

- **That the link was clicked**, and whether from the email or from WhatsApp
- **Each time the Report is opened**, and how many times in total
- **Which sections you read**
- **Whether you downloaded the PDF**
- **Whether you clicked through to the Roadmap**

**What we do with it.** It tells us whether the Report was useful and which parts people actually read, and it tells Ronak when a conversation is likely to be welcome rather than an interruption. If you read your Report four times in a fortnight, we may follow up.

**What we don't do with it.** We don't sell it, we don't share it with anyone outside the people named in §6, and we don't use it to advertise to you elsewhere.

**If you would rather we didn't**, write to bd@corpculture.co and we will stop recording it against your account. Your Report is unaffected.

## 8. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

- **Ask what we hold** about you, and what we have done with it
- **Correct** anything wrong or out of date
- **Have it erased**, where we are not required to keep it
- **Withdraw your consent** at any time, which stops future processing
- **Nominate someone** to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act
- **Complain** to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if we have not resolved it

**Write to bd@corpculture.co.** We will respond within thirty days.

**Withdrawing consent does not undo what has already happened** — a Report already produced and sent remains produced and sent — and it does not remove records the law requires us to keep.

## 10. Security

Your answers are held on servers with access limited to the people in §6. Payment data never reaches our systems. The Report link is long and unguessable, and it is not listed or indexed anywhere.

**A link is only as private as the person holding it.** Anyone you forward it to can read your Report, including every answer you gave us. **Forward the PDF instead** — that is what it is for.

---

## 11. Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep your place in the questionnaire, so you can close the tab and come back without losing your answers.

**There is no account and no password.** You never sign in to read your Report — the private link we send you is what identifies you, which is why §10 asks you not to forward it.

**We do not use advertising cookies**, and we do not let other companies track you across sites from our pages.
