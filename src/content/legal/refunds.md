## Refund Policy

**Corporate Culture** is a brand of **Blue Bird Ventures**. All payments are collected and invoiced by Blue Bird Ventures.

*Last updated: **{{LEGAL_LAST_UPDATED}}***

### What this covers

The **Franchise Readiness Report** (₹1,999 + 18% GST) and the **Franchise Readiness Roadmap** (₹5,999 + 18% GST). The Franchise Readiness Audit is free and nothing is charged for it.

### Before you submit your answers — full refund

**You can cancel and get a full refund at any time before you submit your answers**, whether you have answered none of the questions or all of them.

Until you submit your answers, nothing has been produced and no work has begun. There is no time limit on this and no reason you need to give us. A paid order never expires, so this holds whether you come back the next day or six months later.

### Once you have submitted your answers — no refund

**Once you submit your answers, Ronak begins the review and the purchase cannot be refunded** — except where we miss our own delivery promise, below.

The Report is an assessment. It tells you where your business stands and what would stop a franchise partner signing — and for a good number of brands, the honest answer is that they are not ready yet.

**That answer is the product working, not failing.** We would rather tell you plainly and be of use than tell you what you hoped to hear. We cannot offer refunds to brands who disagree with their assessment, because then we would be selling a conclusion rather than an opinion.

The same applies to the Roadmap.

### If we miss our delivery promise — full refund

We promise your Report within **24 hours on working days** of you submitting your answers. Reports are reviewed on working days, so if you submit on a Saturday or Sunday yours will be with you by Monday evening.

**If we miss that and you would rather have your money back, ask and we will refund you in full** — even if the Report has since been sent.

### If something in your Report is wrong

If your Report contains a **factual error about your business** — a figure misread, an answer recorded incorrectly — tell us. **We will correct it and reissue the Report at no cost.**

This is different from disagreeing with our assessment. An error is something we got wrong about the facts you gave us; an assessment is our judgement of those facts.

### Duplicate and failed payments

**Charged twice, or charged for a payment that failed? We refund it without you having to ask**, as soon as we see it. If you spot one before we do, tell us and it will be back with you quickly.

### How to request a refund

Email **bd@corpculture.co** or WhatsApp **+91 8072 830 857** with the email address or mobile number you used to pay. Nothing else is needed.

### How long it takes

We process approved refunds within **3 working days**. Your bank or card provider then takes its own time — usually **5 to 7 working days** — before the money shows in your account.

Refunds go back to the original payment method wherever possible. If too much time has passed for your payment provider to accept a reversal, we will transfer the amount to your bank account instead.

### GST

Prices are shown exclusive of GST, which is charged at **18%**. Refunds include the GST you paid, and we issue a **credit note** against your original invoice.
